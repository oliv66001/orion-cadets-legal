Legal information and support

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Orion Cadets is a space shooter: you play a student pilot of the Orion Academy defending the human colonies against the Swarm, across 50 levels, varied missions and giant transforming bosses.

Publisher: Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé, France.

The game stores progress, crystals, unlocked ships and settings on the device. It has no account with the publisher. It may show Google AdMob ads and offer in-app purchases through Google Play. If connected to Google Play Games, progress, achievements and scores may be saved by Google (see the privacy policy).
