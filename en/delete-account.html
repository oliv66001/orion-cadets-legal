Account and data deletion

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data of Orion Cadets, published by Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé.

Player account

Orion Cadets does not create any account with the publisher: there is no username, password or profile stored on the publisher’s servers. There is therefore no account to delete on the publisher’s side.

If you use Google Play Games, your cloud save, achievements and scores are linked to your Play Games profile, managed by Google. To delete them: Google Play Games app → Settings → delete your Play Games account and data, or from your Google Account settings.

Delete game data

Progress, crystals, unlocked ships and settings are stored on the device. To erase them:

  • In the game: home screen → Options → Erase my data, then confirm.
  • On Android: phone Settings → Apps → Orion Cadets → Storage → Clear data.

Uninstalling the game also deletes this data. Labels may vary by phone; also consider automatic system backups (Google backup). Purchases made on Google Play remain linked to your Google account and can be restored.

Request deletion of data held by the publisher

If you have written to support, you can request the deletion of your messages and email address:

Request deletion of my data

  • Data deleted: your messages, attachments and email address.
  • Timeframe: within 30 days of receiving your request. A confirmation is sent to you.
  • Possible retention: only what a legal obligation or ongoing dispute requires, and only for as long as necessary.

If an account is added later

If a future version adds a player account, the game will offer account deletion directly in the app, and this page will describe the procedure, the data deleted, the timeframes and any retention.
