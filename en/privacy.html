Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Controller and contact

The data controller is Développement-Solution — Olivier Pagé, 20 rue Condorcet, 66330 Cabestany, France.

For any question about data: developpement.solution@gmail.com.

Data stored by the game

The game keeps on the device: progress (unlocked and completed levels, best scores, simulator times), crystals, unlocked ships and items, the selected ship, and settings (language, music, voice and sound effect volumes, story already seen). This data is not sent to the publisher.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, age or location, and does not access contacts, microphone, camera or files. It has no chat or player-to-player exchange. Voices and music are built into the game: your voice is never recorded.

Google Play Games (cloud save, achievements, leaderboards)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game may send Google your progress (cloud save), unlocked achievements and best scores; Google links them to your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and may show leaderboards to other players according to your visibility settings. The publisher does not receive your email address and does not store this data on its own servers.

This processing is carried out by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited under its privacy policy. Signing in is optional: the game works fully without it.

Advertising, purchases and analytics

Ads. The game may show ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): rewarded videos you choose to watch (continue a game, double earned crystals, start with a bonus) and, at most once every few games, a full-screen ad between two levels. To serve and measure these ads, AdMob processes device data: advertising ID, IP address, technical information and ad interactions, under Google’s advertising policy.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a certified consent form (Google UMP) is shown before any ad. You can refuse personalized ads and change your choice at any time in the game: Options → Ad choices (GDPR). You can also reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone settings (Google → Ads).

In-app purchases. Purchases (no-ads pack, crystals, ships, skins) are processed by Google Play. The publisher receives neither your payment details nor your email address; the game only stores what you bought.

The game includes no other analytics or tracking tool.

External links

Links from the game to these pages open in your browser. GitHub Pages, which hosts these pages, may log technical information (IP address, browser type) in its security logs, under its own privacy policy.

Children

Orion Cadets is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has sent us data (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

Email support

When you contact the publisher, your email address, the content of your message and any attachments are processed to answer your request, handle an issue or exercise your rights. The legal basis is the publisher’s legitimate interest in providing support. This data is accessible to the publisher and the email provider used, and kept as long as needed, then at most three years after the last exchange, unless required by law or a dispute. Do not send passwords or bank details by email.

Retention, deletion and rights

Game data stays on the device until you erase it (in the game or in the phone settings) or uninstall the game: see the account and data deletion page. You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection or portability where applicable law provides, at the address above, and lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Security

The game limits its data to what is described above. Support messages are only accessible to the publisher. No system is completely risk-free; do not send unnecessary sensitive data.

Changes

This policy may change, especially if a player account or online mode is added. The date at the top of the page will be updated and, for significant changes, it will be announced in the game or on the Google Play listing.
